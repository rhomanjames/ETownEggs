Trade What You Have for Fresh Eggs

ETown Eggs is a small family-run farm serving the Elizabethtown community. We believe local food should strengthen local relationships.

Instead of only accepting cash, we're happy to barter for useful goods, handmade items, garden produce, tools, services, and other items that help support a small farm and local families.

Let's Trade

Why Barter?

Bartering keeps resources circulating within our community. It helps neighbors support one another while reducing waste and creating meaningful local connections. Every trade tells a story and strengthens our local economy.

Our goal: Build a stronger community where fresh food, useful skills, and quality goods can be exchanged directly between neighbors.
